A mirror for your meetings.

Mirona records your calls, transcribes them on your Mac, works out who said what, and keeps every meeting as Markdown files you own. Claude and other AI agents can search them, if you let them.

What it does

Calls are where work is promised. Mirona remembers it.

Decisions and action items fade by the next day. Mirona keeps them, linked to the moment they were said.

Private by default

Speech-to-text, speaker detection and search run on your Mac. One switch blocks every cloud service.

Files you own

One folder per meeting: meeting.md and transcript.jsonl. Back them up, sync them, grep them.

Useful afterwards

Action items, decisions and open questions, tagged by project, client or anything you like. Summaries follow templates you can edit, or add as your own meeting types.

Knows who spoke

Speakers are split after the call. Name a voice once, from its clearest lines, and Mirona recognises it in later meetings.

Search by meaning

Find a meeting by keywords or by what was meant, across everything you have recorded. All local.

Ready for your agents

An MCP server, a CLI and a Claude Code skill. Off until you turn it on.

Privacy

Your meetings stay on your Mac.

What people say in a call is theirs too. Mirona is built so nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose a cloud model.

  • Privacy mode, on from the startEvery network request goes through one gate. In privacy mode only your Mac and your own network get through.
  • No telemetryNo analytics, no accounts. Update checks are opt-in.
  • Encrypted at rest, if you wantTranscripts, audio and voiceprints, with the key in your keychain.
  • Retention rulesDelete audio or whole meetings after the time you choose.
Sensitive lines stay out

Passwords, salaries and account numbers are tagged on your Mac. They stay out of summaries, cloud models, search and agents, which see [redacted] unless you allow it.

For your agents

Ask Claude what the client agreed.

Mirona serves your meetings over MCP, on stdio or localhost with a token per client. Agents can search meetings, read transcripts, list action items and ask questions about a call. Turn it on in Settings › Agents (MCP).

CLI and MCP guide →

# Claude Code, with the app installed
claude mcp add mirona -- \
  /Applications/Mirona.app/Contents/MacOS/mirona-app mcp

# or from the terminal
mirona search "insurer grouping"
mirona actions
Get started

Recording in three steps.

Download

Get the latest DMG from GitHub, open it and drag Mirona to Applications.

Set up

Allow the microphone and system audio. Mirona downloads the speech and speaker models once (about 1 GB).

Record

From the sidebar, the menu bar, or ⌘⌥⇧R from any app. ⌘⌥⇧M mutes you mid-call. Summaries use Ollama on your Mac by default, or on another computer on your network.
