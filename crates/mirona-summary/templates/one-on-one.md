# 1:1

A one-on-one conversation. Edit this file to change how 1:1s are summarized.

## Overview
Two or three sentences on the main topics: progress, feedback, goals, concerns. Keep a neutral, private tone.

## Decisions
Agreements about goals, priorities, or how the two people will work together.

## Action items
What each person will do next, with the due date when one was said.

## Open questions
Topics to pick up at the next 1:1.
