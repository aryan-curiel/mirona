# Planning

A planning session (sprint, roadmap, project). Edit this file to change how planning meetings are summarized.

## Overview
Two to four sentences: the goal of the period being planned and the main things that are in or out.

## Decisions
What was committed to, dropped, or reprioritized, and any estimates that were agreed.

## Action items
Tasks that were assigned, with the owner and the due date when one was said.

## Open questions
Risks, unknowns, and estimates that still need an answer.
