# Standup

A short daily check-in. Edit this file to change how standups are summarized.

## Overview
Two or three sentences: what the team is working on and whether anything is blocked.

## Decisions
Only real agreements, such as a change of plan or priority. Status updates are not decisions.

## Action items
Follow-ups someone took on, with the owner and the due date when one was said. Blockers that someone agreed to clear count as action items.

## Open questions
Blockers or questions nobody answered during the call.
