# Code of conduct

Mirona follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people, and
don't harass anyone, in public or in private. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions
and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/aryan-curiel/mirona/security/advisories/new)
(mark it as a conduct report), or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block people who break these rules, as
described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
